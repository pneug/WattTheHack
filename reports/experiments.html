Reconstructing turbine wind speed: experiment results

About 40 configurations tested on 28 simulated 3-day outages plus the 4–9 Sep validation window.

TL;DR
  • Energy error per turbine and outage fell from 13.8 % to 10.0 % (27 % relative) with the final model E12_dir8_smooth60_power.
  • The baseline underestimated energy by 8.6 / 5.4 / 7.2 % (T1/T2/T3); the final model's bias is +2.3 / +1.5 / +2.2 %.
  • Gradient boosting reaches 10.8 %, the transparent final model 10.0 %; 95 % band coverage is 94.8 % (baseline 94.6 %).
